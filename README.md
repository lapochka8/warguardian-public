# WarGuardian

A hosted RCON panel for **WARDOGS** dedicated servers. Moderate players, share ban lists with
other communities, give each staff member their own sign-in and review evidence before a ban.
It runs in your browser. Your game server stays with your current host, and there is nothing to
install.

**One server is always free, with every feature.** Paid plans from $5 a month add more servers.
See [pricing](https://warguardian.app/pricing).

## What you get

| Area | What staff can do |
| --- | --- |
| Live players | See who is on the server, their faction and match stats. |
| Moderation | Warn, message, kick and ban, including timed bans and bans for players who already left. |
| Shared bans | Keep one ban list for your community and share it with communities you trust. |
| Staff access | Staff sign in with Discord. You choose what each person can do on each server. Every action is logged. |
| Player history | Names, visits, notes, past bans and Steam, VAC and game-ban info for each player. |
| Evidence review | Kill feed, combat stats and weapon comparisons to check a report before you ban. |
| Server controls | Maps, rotations, match settings, whitelist and priority queue. |
| Automation | Team balance, high-ping and idle kicks, name and tag rules, team-kill limits, seeding protection and rewards, announcements and restart warnings. |
| Reports | A ticket inbox for player reports, with assignment, replies and private staff notes. |
| Discord and API | Staff alerts in Discord, plus an API and MCP access for your own bots and scripts. |
| Public pages | Optional leaderboards, player careers and a report link for your players. |

Your RCON password stays on our servers and never reaches a staff member's browser.

## Connect your first server

1. Open [warguardian.app](https://warguardian.app/) and sign in with Discord.
2. Create your community.
3. Get the RCON address, port and password from your game host. The RCON port is often not the
   game port.
4. Follow the [connection guide](https://warguardian.app/wardogs-rcon/setup) and test the
   connection.
5. Invite your staff and pick their roles.

If the connection fails, ask in [support](SUPPORT.md). Remove the password from any error
message before you post it.
